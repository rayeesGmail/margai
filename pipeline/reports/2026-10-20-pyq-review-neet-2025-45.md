The review README §7 asks for before the founder's approval: every flag of the extract run and all 180 questions read
against the booklet's page images, and the answers filled from NTA's final key. Draft:
`pipeline/data/pyq/neet-2025-45.draft.json` from `pyq extract` (report `2026-10-20-pyq-extract.md`: 45 pages, 180
questions, every section at its declared count, no page below 0.80 confidence, ₹150.16). Reviewed file:
`pipeline/data/pyq/neet-2025-45.reviewed.json` — it enters `pipeline/inputs/papers/neet-2025-45.json` only on the
founder's approval. It is written in the draft's own layout (PaperFileJson's — proven 2026-10-21 by reading it with
PaperFileJson and writing it back, byte for byte; until then each `answer` stood after `extraction`, the writer's
order reversed), so `diff` of draft against reviewed shows exactly the corrections below — as the cross-check of 2026-10-21 amended them (Q173 reverted, Q61 added; see
the Result) — and the answers. Two things in the extract report come from a jar older than this
review: its `read:` line says "nothing yet" (the register's count is printed since 10c24ac), and its last line names
the draft by an absolute path (fixed for later runs in 558113e; a committed report is never edited). The pages were
drawn again afterwards and the images kept, with no model call (`2026-10-20-pyq-extract-2.md` kept all 45, `-3.md`
drew all 45 again to the same bytes) — re-renders, not provably the bytes the calls read (DECISIONS 2026-10-20); the

## How it was read

- **Every question, character by character, against the English column of its page.** Five reviewers split the 45
  pages (2–10, 11–19, 20–28, 29–37, 38–46), each reading at zoom 2.5 and at 4–10 for small glyphs, superscripts,
  structures and figures, and filing every difference with the exact span.
- **Claude checked every filed difference on the page image before accepting it** — and Q1, Q2, Q3, Q174 besides.
  Nothing was changed on a reviewer's word alone. The spec audit of this review then held every correction to the
  notation `pyq_extract.v1` writes and the NCERT corpus spells (Q65, Q104 and Q173 among the corrections — Q173's
  later reverted by the cross-check — and Q174 among the close calls).
- **Then on the saved page images** (the founder's acceptance, DECISIONS 2026-10-20): every figure flag and every
  correction against the saved 150-dpi re-renders, and all 45 pages swept for strokes the scan lost (sections below).
  Each local copy (`pipeline/data/pyq/pages/neet-2025-45/`) was checked against the sha256 table of
  `2026-10-20-pyq-extract-2.md`: 45 of 45 match. These images are re-renders — not provably the bytes the calls read;
  DECISIONS 2026-10-20 says how far they are.
- **The booklet is a scan.** Pages 2–46 are each one 1-bit JBIG2 image of 1240 × 1753 pixels (150 dpi), except page
  19 (Q61–Q64), an 8-bit JPEG of 595 × 841 (72 dpi). The saved images, drawn at 150 dpi, are 1239 × 1752 — the
  JBIG2 scan resampled by one row and one column, and page 19's JPEG enlarged about two times — so they carry the scan's detail, and nothing finer than 150 dpi (72 on page
  19) exists in the file for any drawing to show; a zoom only enlarges. The lost strokes below were confirmed on the
  native scan itself for Q3, Q7 and Q65, and against NTA's Hindi column for Q7, Q14 and Q90.

## Strokes the scan lost (transcribed as meant)

A gap is not a misprint: a misprint is a wrong character printed (Q14's "N/m", printed so in both columns), a gap is a
character whose ink is missing (DECISIONS 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-21, Claude's rule, for the founder to overrule; where
NTA's Hindi column prints the character whole, the English is read as that character — a test of meaning, not of
cause, which would also correct an English-only typesetting slip). *Since 2026-10-21 (the founder; DECISIONS), an
English-only misprint the paper prints is kept as printed with a note, by SPEC §6.2, in later papers; that changes
nothing here — Q7's "sin 0" was the scan's (the second official copy, lossless, prints "sin θ"), and the places below
are strokes missing from the ink, which the 2026-10-20 gap rule reads as meant and the decision leaves as they are.* This booklet's 1-bit scan loses thin strokes
throughout — superscript minus signs, the crossbars of A, H, F, italic t and θ, the base of Δ, parts of "+" — so all
45 pages were swept on the saved images for every such place: **112 places in 63 questions — 111 read as meant by the
extraction, and one copied as printed, Q7 option 4's "sin 0", corrected above; none unclear** — each with what the
context fixes it by (the other options, the given data, units and labels printed whole elsewhere, the Hindi column).
